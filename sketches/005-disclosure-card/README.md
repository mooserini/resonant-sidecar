## Variant: Disclosure Card

### Design stance
Make the privacy boundary the opening product explanation, then let it recede once the user understands it.

### Key choices
- Layout: large current-page disclosure card above a deliberately quiet conversation area.
- Typography: warm product type with compact mono overlines.
- Color: layered charcoal, brown, copper, and semantic green; magenta indicates selected disclosure scope.
- Interaction: a modal combines scope, lifetime, readable preview, and explicit send; camera capture appears as a concrete visual attachment.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: first-run comprehension, trust, and explaining the difference between page identity and page contents.
- Weak at: the opening card consumes more vertical room and may feel instructional after the model is familiar.

### Best for
- Onboarding and users who reasonably distrust opaque ambient page access.
